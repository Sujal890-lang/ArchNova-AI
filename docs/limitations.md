# ArchNova AI — Project Limitations & Responsible-Use Guidelines

## 1. Conceptual Design Scope
ArchNova AI is built as an early-stage architectural generative modeling, volumetric massing, and spatial budgeting application. It is **not** a licensed building certification system or professional construction document generator.

## 2. Regulatory & Statutory Planning Limitations
- **Zoning Assumptions**: All setback rules, Floor Area Ratio (FAR/FSI) caps, and maximum building height thresholds checked by ArchNova AI are derived solely from user-specified numerical parameters.
- **No Statutory Verification**: ArchNova AI does not automatically fetch or verify local municipal development control regulations (DCR), zoning overlay maps, conservation area bylaws, or coastal zone management rules.
- **Setback Encroachments**: While setback clearances are evaluated mathematically, specific variance exceptions or easements (e.g. utility right-of-way, fire engine access turns) require municipal surveyor sign-off.

## 3. Structural & Engineering Limitations
- **No Structural Load Analysis**: The parametric building generator creates geometric solids and architectural envelopes. It does not calculate dead, live, wind, or seismic structural loads.
- **Cantilever & Column Sizing**: Balcony cantilevers and pergola post spans are stylized visual approximations and must be engineered by a licensed civil or structural engineer.
- **MEP & Building Services**: Floor plans generate preliminary spatial partitions (Living, Dining, Kitchen, Bedroom, Bath). HVAC ducting, plumbing stacks, electrical risers, and drainage slopes must be detailed in dedicated engineering phases.

## 4. Responsible AI Usage
ArchNova Copilot is designed to interpret natural-language requests into structured parametric inputs. Users must review all extracted values before proceeding to construction budgeting or municipal submissions.
